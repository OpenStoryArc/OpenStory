1 Two planes — attribution is not a security boundary

The same IDs flow through two different systems. One labels what was observed (spoofable). The other enforces who may see or act (the real boundaries). Conflating them is the trap.

attribution — self-asserted label (not a boundary) enforcement — real boundary, fail-closed
