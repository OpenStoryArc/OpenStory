The reading system

Kindle's page, abstracted into rules OpenStory can apply. This document is set in the system it describes.

Four papers

Every theme is a paper with a name. Sepia is the default; Black is what the system picks when your machine is dark. Ink, secondary ink, rule and accent are chosen per paper so the accent stays above 3:1 and the secondary ink above 4.5:1 on all four. Pick one in the header and this page turns to it.

Two fonts, strictly divided

Anything a person reads is set in a book serif. Bookerly is Amazon's and cannot be licensed, so the stack is Charter, then Iowan Old Style, then Georgia; each ships on the machines OpenStory runs on, and none of them needs downloading. This paragraph is justified with hyphenation, the way a Kindle sets a novel, at seventeen pixels on a sixteen-pixel root with a line-height of 1.6 and a measure of thirty-eight ems, which is about sixty-six characters. Nothing else on a reading surface is allowed to be wider than this.

Secondary text lives in one band: this shade and no other. It is used for the fact about the fact, never for the fact itself.

Five steps, not fourteen

  • Display, for a reel's title beat2rem
  • Title, for a card or a section1.375rem
  • Reading, the body of any story1.0625rem
  • Instrument text, for rows and facets0.8125rem
  • Caption, for footers and timestamps0.75rem

The S, M, L and XL control changes the root from 15 to 20 pixels; the steps stay where they are.

Marginalia, not overlays

Four tints, all desaturated, all readable under ink. On Black paper they darken instead of lighten. A sentence that recurs across sessions gets the dotted underline with a count, the way Kindle marks a popular passage.

The mark

An open book as one closed silhouette. The spine is the mirror axis: the right page is the left page reflected. The gutter is a hairline slit in the outline that vanishes at sixteen pixels and appears at sixty-four and above. One path, one color, no gradient.

In the header it sits at twenty pixels beside the wordmark, in ink, in the reading serif. The file is docs/design/openstory-mark.svg.
